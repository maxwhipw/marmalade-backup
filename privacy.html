marmalade-backup — privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner.

It uses Google Drive's "drive.file" permission, which lets it create and read only the backup files it made itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files.

Backups are encrypted on the owner's computers before they are uploaded. No data is collected from, shared with, or sold to anyone.
